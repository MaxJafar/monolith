## Start writing

Journal is included with Monolith. Open **Journal** from the sidebar, choose **New entry**, enter a title and date, then continue writing in the native page editor.

Entries are workspace records. They remain available in Docs, database views and Calendar when dated. Editing an entry updates that same record.

## Find an entry

Use search to find titles, descriptions or tags. Filter by month or clear the date filter to browse every entry, newest first. The activity strip summarizes writing days and entries.

## Sharing and saved work

Workspace roles apply to Journal. Viewers can read accessible entries; owners and editors can create and edit them. A failed creation keeps the draft open, and navigation asks before discarding unsaved changes.

Calendar, Journal and Projects use native Monolith implementations. Their retired addon packages are no longer installed or loaded. Existing workspace records are retained. Historical addon files remain separate from the application.
